Common questions

How do I send a countdown?

Open a conversation in Messages, tap the + button, choose ChatDays, enter an event name and date, pick your colours and tap Insert into message. Then send it as usual. If ChatDays isn't in the list, tap More and turn it on.

What is the difference between Live and Static?

A Live countdown keeps itself up to date: the number of days changes each day for everyone who has ChatDays. A Static countdown is a fixed picture of the countdown as it was when you sent it, and it works for everyone.

My friend doesn't have ChatDays. What do they see?

They see a card with the event and date and a prompt to get the app. If they tap it, they'll see the live countdown on this website, which works in any browser.

My countdown number looks out of date.

Live countdowns update at midnight in the viewer's own time zone. Make sure the countdown was sent as Live and that the viewer has ChatDays installed. If a Live countdown still looks wrong, contact us with the event date and your iOS version.

Why do people in other time zones see a different number of days?

The event date is the same for everyone, but each person counts down from their own calendar day.

Can I use ChatDays with Android or WhatsApp?

ChatDays works inside iMessage on iPhone. Countdown links open in any browser, so someone on Android can still view a countdown you send them.

Can I edit or update a countdown I already sent?

Not at the moment. Send a new countdown instead.

What data does ChatDays collect?

None. See the privacy policy.
