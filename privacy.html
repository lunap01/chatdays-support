Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

ChatDays lets you create countdowns and share them in your Messages conversations. This policy explains what the ChatDays app and this website do and do not do with your information. The short version: we don't run servers for the app, you don't create an account, and we don't collect your data.

The ChatDays app

  • No account. You don't register or sign in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The app doesn't include analytics or advertising tools, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.
  • No developer servers. The app doesn't send your countdowns, contacts or conversations to us. We never see them.
  • No access to your conversations or contacts. ChatDays only does what Apple's Messages extension allows: it adds the countdown you create to the conversation you are in. It doesn't read other messages or collect information about the other people in the conversation.
  • On your device. The app may keep settings and purchase status on your device, such as how many countdowns you have sent and whether you have unlocked the full version. This stays on your device.

What is in a countdown you send

When you send a countdown, its details (the event name, date, optional emoji and the colours you chose) are included in the message so that the people you send it to can see it. The message travels through Apple's Messages service to the people in the conversation, under Apple's own privacy terms. We don't receive a copy.

Purchases

If you buy the full version, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We don't see or store your payment details.

This website

If someone without the app taps a countdown they receive, they may be taken to this website. The page works out the countdown inside the visitor's own browser. The countdown details are carried in the part of the web address after the # sign, which web browsers do not send to the website's server. The website has no analytics, no cookies and no forms.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record standard technical information about visits, such as IP addresses, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We don't have access to that information.

Children

ChatDays doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
